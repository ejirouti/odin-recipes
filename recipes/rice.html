Rice dish

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Cook the rice according to package instructions.
  2. Brown the ground beef and drain excess fat.
  3. Mix the ricotta cheese with eggs and seasoning.
  4. Layer the rice, meat sauce, and cheese mixture in a baking dish.
  5. Bake at 375°F for 30 minutes or until bubbly.
